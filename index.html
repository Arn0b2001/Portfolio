Undergraduate Thesis · 2024

Supervisor: Dr. Farig Sadeque

Python RAG LLaMA-3.3-70B ChromaDB BM25 Sentence-Transformers

Transforming Mentorship — An AI-Powered Chatbot Approach to University Guidance

Developed a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)-based chatbot to assist BRAC University students with academic queries and course planning. Built a hybrid retrieval pipeline combining lexical (BM25) and semantic (ChromaDB + sentence-transformers) search, integrated with LLaMA-3.3-70B for natural, personalized responses.

BLEU: 0.517 ROUGE-L: 0.727 BERTScore: 0.831 METEOR: 0.809

Preprint · 2025

Python PyTorch Deep Clustering t-SNE

Unsupervised Deep Clustering of MNIST with Triplet-Enhanced Convolutional Autoencoders

Proposed a two-phase deep clustering model combining convolutional autoencoders with triplet loss for unsupervised representation learning. Jointly optimized reconstruction loss and clustering-oriented triplet loss to improve cluster compactness and separation in the latent space. Visualized embeddings using t-SNE to demonstrate clear separation of digit classes.

Metrics: ARI · NMI · Silhouette Visualization: t-SNE
